# Customer_Shopping_Behavior
I recently developed an interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard in Power BI to analyze customer purchasing patterns, subscription trends, revenue generation, and demographic insights. The dashboard transforms raw customer data into meaningful business intelligence, helping organizations make data-driven decisions.
The analysis includes 599 customers with an average purchase amount of $60.26 and an average review rating of 3.79. Key performance indicators (KPIs) provide a quick overview of customer engagement and purchasing behavior.
One of the major insights from the dashboard is the subscription status analysis, where a larger percentage of customers are non-subscribers compared to subscribers. This information can help businesses design targeted marketing campaigns to increase subscription conversions and customer retention.
The dashboard also highlights revenue and sales performance by product category, allowing businesses to identify their top-performing segments. In the current view, the Footwear category contributes significantly to overall sales and revenue.
Additionally, customer demographics were analyzed through age-group segmentation. The findings show that the Middle-Aged customer group generates the highest revenue and sales, followed by Young Adults. These insights can help companies focus their marketing efforts on the most profitable customer segments.
Interactive filters for Gender, Subscription Status, Category, and Shipping Type allow users to explore customer behavior from multiple perspectives and uncove deeper insights.
